How It Works

Get in Touch

2

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our commission painting services

Yes, we always accept painting commissions! You can contact us through our contact form, email, or social media channels. We work on projects of all sizes, from single models to entire armies.

Turnaround time depends on the quantity and complexity of models. Typically, smaller projects (up to 20 models) take 2-4 weeks, while larger armies may take 8+ weeks. We'll provide an estimated completion date when you receive your quote.

Absolutely! We can order models directly to our studio, which often saves you time and shipping costs. In many cases, we can also offer discounts on miniatures through our supplier partnerships.

We accept PayPal, bank transfers, and credit cards. For larger projects, we typically require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the balance due upon completion before shipping.

Yes, we ship worldwide! Shipping costs are calculated based on destination and package weight. All models are carefully packaged with protective materials to ensure they arrive safely.
